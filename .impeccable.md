# PDF Master — design direction

**Direction:** Apple macOS Sequoia / iPadOS Freeform & Preview precision glass studio.
**One line:** the page is the hero; every control is a translucent instrument floating a few millimetres above it, and nothing competes with the document.

This file is the contract for anything visual. If a change does not fit here, change this file first.

---

## 1. Personality

| Trait | It means | It never means |
|---|---|---|
| **Precise** | Millimetre truth, crisp 1 px borders, tabular numbers, calibrated scales. | Clinical, grey, spreadsheet. |
| **Calm** | One accent colour, quiet motion, generous space around the sheet. | Empty, low contrast, hiding controls. |
| **Tactile** | Glass, soft inner light, paper-like shadows, real hover/press states. | Skeuomorphic leather, fake stitching, embossed bevels. |
| **Confident** | Controls sit where a professional's hand expects them; labels are verbs. | Shouting, badges everywhere, decoration as hierarchy. |
| **Alive** | Depth comes from light (glows, blur, saturation), not from more boxes. | Neon, animation for its own sake. |

Voice: short, literal, no exclamation marks. "Save as stamp", not "Save your stamp!". Units and numbers are shown as values (`1:50`, `300 DPI`), never explained in prose inside a control.

---

## 2. Spatial system

- **Frame:** 8 px outer margin around every surface. Panels are `m-2`, corners `rounded-2xl` (16 px).
- **Radii:** 16 px panels, 18 px tool rail, 12 px pills/fields, 10 px icon buttons, 999 px chips, 2 px paper (paper is paper).
- **Density:** 26 px chips, 28–32 px icon buttons, 34 px pills, 56 px top bar, 13 px base text (11.5 px in chips, 10.5 px in status metadata).
- **Spacing:** 4 px grid; 6 px between icon buttons, 8 px between groups, 12 px inside panels.
- **Elevation:** three steps only — `--shadow-soft` (hover), `--shadow` (floating chrome), `--paper-shadow` (the sheet). Never invent a fourth.

---

## 3. Colour

Three themes, one token set. Accent is system blue in light/dark, burnt sienna in sepia; nothing else is allowed to be saturated.

| Token | Light | Dark | Sepia | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `--bg` | `#dfe5ef` | `#090b10` | `#efe4cf` | App backdrop |
| `--glass` | `rgba(255,255,255,.58)` | dark glass | warm glass | Panels, toolbars |
| `--glass-strong` | `.78` | stronger | stronger | Popovers, floating bars |
| `--fg` | `#14161b` | near-white | `#3a2f22` | Text |
| `--muted` | `#6a7280` | grey | `#8a775e` | Secondary text, idle icons |
| `--border` | 10 % ink | 2–3 % white | 14 % ink | Every hairline |
| `--accent` | `#0a84ff` | `#0a84ff` | `#b0552a` | Selection, active tool, focus |
| `--paper` | `#fffdf8` | `#f7f7f5` | `#fffaf0` | The document sheet |
| `--danger` / `--success` | `#ff453a` / `#30d158` | same | `#b3261e` / `#4c7a34` | Destructive / confirmation |

Rules:
1. Max **one** accent-filled surface in view at a time (active tool, or primary button).
2. Ink for annotation colour comes from the document's palette, never from the chrome palette.
3. Glows (`--glow-1..3`) are ambient light behind the sheet. They never sit on a control.
4. Text on glass must clear 4.5:1; `--muted` is for metadata, not for the only label of a control.
5. `color-mix(in srgb, var(--accent) 24%, transparent)` is the standard accent tint — do not hand-pick new alphas.

---

## 4. Type

- Family: `Inter Variable`, fallback SF Pro / Segoe UI. No second family, no display serif.
- Scale: 10.5 metadata · 11.5 chips/labels · 13 body · 15 panel titles · 18 dialog titles. No 20+ except empty-state headings.
- Weight: 500 for controls, 600 for titles, 400 for prose. Never 700+.
- Numbers that move (page count, zoom, measurements, dimensions) use tabular figures.
- Labels are sentence case. Micro-labels may be ALL CAPS at 10 px with 0.06 em tracking — maximum two per panel.

---

## 5. Light, glass, paper

- Glass = translucent fill + 1 px `--border` + `--shadow` + `inset 0 1px 0 var(--edge)` + `backdrop-filter: blur(26px) saturate(170%)` (`34px` for `.glass-strong`).
- Blur is for chrome over the canvas. Never blur the paper or anything inside it.
- The sheet gets `--paper-shadow` and a 2 px radius; it is the only object allowed a large shadow.
- Paper recipes (129 parametric styles) are drawn as SVG ink, never as background images.
- No gradients on controls. Gradients belong to the ambient glows and the app icon only.

---

## 6. Motion

- Durations: 120–160 ms for state, 220–260 ms for entrances (`anim-in`), 0 ms for measurement readouts.
- Easing: `ease` / `ease-out`. No bounce, no spring, no overshoot on chrome.
- What moves: panel entrance (opacity + 6 px rise), toast, popover origin, selection handles. What does not: the sheet during zoom (transform only), ink while drawing, text while typing.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` collapses everything to 0.01 ms (already enforced globally).
- Print is motionless: `@media print` hides `.top-bar`, `.sidebar`, `.tool-rail`, `.inspector`, `.status-line`, `.selection-chrome`, `.snapshot-bar`, `.crop-overlay`, `.presentation-bar` and flattens `.viewport`, `.page-stage`, `.page-paper`.

---

## 7. Component grammar

- **Icon button:** Lucide, 16 px, `strokeWidth 1.75`, `--muted` → `--fg` on hover, accent tint + accent icon when `aria-pressed`.
- **Chip:** pill, 26 px, transparent with hairline; active state is accent tint, never solid accent.
- **Segmented control:** track (`--track`) with a 24 px thumb per option; the chosen option is the only filled child.
- **Tool rail:** vertical glass column, groups separated by a 24 px hairline, tooltip to the right with name + shortcut.
- **Panels:** header row (icon + title + close), scroll body, footer actions. Hairlines between regions, never cards inside cards.
- **Popovers:** `.glass-strong`, 16 px radius, 8 px offset from trigger, arrowless.
- **Toast:** bottom centre, 10 px from the edge, auto-dismiss, one line, no icon except on failure.
- **Focus:** 2 px `--accent` outline at 2 px offset on `:focus-visible` only.

Every icon-only control carries `aria-label` and `title`. Every toggle carries `aria-pressed`.

---

## 8. Anti-patterns (reject on sight)

- More than one accent colour, or accent used for decoration.
- Solid opaque panels on the canvas; a second card inside a panel.
- Pure `#000` text or `#fff` panels in light theme; pure `#fff` text in dark theme.
- Drop shadows on text, borders on borders, or radii mixed inside one row.
- Bouncy springs, blur-in animations, skeleton shimmer.
- Sentence-length helper text inside a toolbar. Help belongs in tooltips, empty states, or Settings.
- New bespoke button/input classes when `.chip`, `.icon-btn`, `.pill`, `.seg` already cover it.
- Hiding the page behind chrome; the sheet must always keep at least 60 % of the canvas width at 1440 px.

---

## 9. Review checklist

1. Does it read at a glance in all three themes?
2. Is the page still the brightest, largest object?
3. One accent, one shadow step, one radius family?
4. Keyboard reachable, labelled, focus-visible?
5. Does it survive `@media print` and `prefers-reduced-motion`?
6. Would removing it lose information? If not, remove it.
